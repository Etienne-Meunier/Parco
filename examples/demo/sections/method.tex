\section{Method}

We consider the advection equation
\begin{equation}
\partial_t u + c\,\partial_x u = 0, \qquad u(x, 0) = u_0(x),
\label{eq:advection}
\end{equation}
discretised in time with the $\theta$-method, where $\theta \in [0, 1]$ controls the implicitness.

The amplification factor of the scheme is $g(k) = \frac{1 - (1-\theta)\, i \lambda \sin k}{1 + \theta\, i \lambda \sin k}$, so $|g| \le 1$ whenever $\theta \ge 1/2$.
