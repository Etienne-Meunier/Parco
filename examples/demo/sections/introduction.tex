\section{Introduction}

Advection dominated problems are ubiquitous in geophysical fluid dynamics \cite{parco}.
Explicit schemes are limited by the \emph{Courant--Friedrichs--Lewy} condition, which forces very small time steps when the velocity is large.

%parco explain why implicit schemes avoid the CFL restriction

Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
\item a semi-implicit discretisation of the advection operator;
\item a stability analysis valid for any time step;
\item numerical experiments on a idealised basin.
\end{itemize}
